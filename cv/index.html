Profile

Formal Verification Projects

Selected collaborative contributions to Verified-zkEVM and mathlib. Some formalizations used Aristotle (Harmonic). The Formal Proof page gives mathematical details, sources, and contribution statuses.

Computable polynomial algebra — CompPoly and mathlib

Contributed polynomial representation equivalences and correctness interfaces, bivariate evaluation, finite-field and additive-NTT lemmas, and fixed-domain barycentric interpolation. Corrected evaluation assumptions and monic-division early stopping, with regression tests. Extended mathlib’s single-variable polynomial equivalence and coefficient/evaluation APIs.

CompPoly contributions · Barycentric interpolation

Coding theory — ArkLib

Contributed Johnson-bound and Reed–Solomon proximity-gap formalizations, correcting missing hypotheses and degenerate cases. Added correctness and inconsistency proofs for the noncomputable Berlekamp–Welch linear-solver specification. Parts of the Guruswami–Sudan work were incorporated upstream.

Johnson-bound and decoder specifications · Proximity-gap proofs

Probabilistic proof interfaces — VCVio and PolyFun

Contributed round-by-round extraction and probabilistic knowledge-transition interfaces, random-oracle query-budget tests and countermodels separating binding, component soundness and relation refinement. Machine-counting and state-normalization proof contributions were incorporated into PolyFun’s complexity backend.

Knowledge and extraction interfaces · PolyFun integration

Proof-system algebra — leanerVM

Contributed mixed-ring evaluation of aligned stacks, padding-aware multilinear identities, and public index and bytecode-column evaluators with correctness proofs and boundary tests. Integrated upstream alongside other contributors’ modules.

Upstream integration and contributor credits
