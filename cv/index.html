Curriculum Vitae

Research mathematician and engineer, based in Cape Town, South Africa.

Research

Preprint: Wouter van Doorn, Elias Judin, Pietro Monticone, and Daniel Morrison. On Some Problems from the Kourovka Notebook. July 2026; revised 26 July 2026. Solutions to eight group-theory problems, discovered and formally verified in Lean by Aristotle, Harmonic’s formal reasoning agent.

Paper and collaborators · arXiv · PDF · Lean source

Selected formalization contributions: Lean work on coding theory in ArkLib, polynomial interpolation in CompPoly, and random-oracle and extraction controls in VCVio. The formal proof portfolio gives the contributions, integration records, attribution, and ongoing research.

Academic history

2023 – present (expected 2026): MSc Mathematics, University of Cape Town.

Thesis (in progress): Semirings, Semimodules, and Semialgebras. Proves an equivalence between varieties with zero morphisms in which composition preserves addition on hom-sets and categories of semimodules over semirings.

Supervisors: Prof. George Janelidze and Dr Juana Sanchez-Ortega.

2022: BSc (Hons) Mathematics (cum laude), University of Cape Town.

Honours project: Free Semimodules and Their Examples. Supervisor: Prof. George Janelidze.

2019 – 2021: BSc Mathematics and Computer Science (cum laude), University of the Witwatersrand.

2014 – 2018: IEB National Senior Certificate, King David Victory Park High School.
