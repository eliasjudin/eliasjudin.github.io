Sheaves and presheaves

Facets of descent I by George Janelidze & Walter Tholen.

Sheaves are presheaves with an equalizer diagram

The Yoneda embedding Y:Open(X)→SetOpen(X)op sends each open set to its representable presheaf in the ambient presheaf category.

There is an adjunction L⊣Γ:SetOpen(X)op⇄(Top↓X) , where L forms the étalé space (sheafification) of a presheaf and Γ assigns to a space over X its presheaf of sections.

Restricting this adjunction to sheaves and local homeomorphisms yields the classical equivalence Shv(X)≃Etale(X) .

Given an open cover {Ui}i∈I of U , the restriction morphism δ:F(U)→∏i∈IF(Ui) records the sections on each element of the cover.

The matching condition is encoded by the parallel morphisms α,β:∏i∈IF(Ui)→∏(i,j)∈I×IF(Ui∩Uj) defined by α((si))=(si|Ui∩Uj)) and β((si))=(sj|Ui∩Uj)) .

The sheaf condition asserts that F makes the above diagram an equalizer: compatible families glue uniquely to a section on U .
